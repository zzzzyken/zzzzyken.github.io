Experience

  • Jul – Nov 2026

    Nashville, TN, USA

    Research: Domain Generalization in Diabetic Retinopathy Grading
    Vanderbilt University, Medical Image Analysis
    Advisors: Prof. Yuankai Huo and Prof. He Zhao
    • Developed a dual-scale lesion and background prototype framework using severity labels alone, combining competitive assignment with severity-guided multiple instance learning.
    • Designed a 19-parameter additive ordinal grading head driven exclusively by 14 lesion prototype presence scores, with teacher guided degradation consistency and spatial shortcut regularization.
    • Achieved mean quadratic weighted kappa (QWK) of 0.761 across six held-out domains in leave-one-domain-out evaluation, surpassing the strongest evaluated baseline by 0.036 (three seeds).
    • Validated lesion localization and decision contributions through prototype removal; improved grading in five existing prototype architectures by transferring the supervision strategy.
